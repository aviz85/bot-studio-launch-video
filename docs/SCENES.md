# Scene briefs (shot ideas)

Frames are global: `B(k) = round(k × 26.8657)`. These are starting ideas. Each scene agent should beat them, while keeping the story, the copy and the beat sync.

## s01 · Black box · beats 0–16 (frames 0–430) · intro, no kick
- **0–4:** Darkness and haze. A slow push toward a lone, dull, flat-grey "old chatbot" widget adrift in space ("How can I help you today?" with typing dots). Projector-like beam, dust, faint HUD timecode. Copy at beat 2: "You know chatbots."
- **4–8:** Low angle: the widget is dwarfed by a colossal **monolith of prompt text** (tiny instructions scrolling) rising behind it. Crane up along the monolith.
- **8–12:** "One giant prompt.", one word per beat (8, 9, 10). Hard cuts to new angles of the monolith (top-down, Dutch, extreme macro of the text). Soft impacts only; the music is quiet.
- **12–16:** The monolith corrupts: RGB split, slice glitches, "Why did it say that?" glitch-typed. It ends in an unsettling stutter and darkness.
- **SFX:** holo hum bed, data chatter, low pulses, glitches at 12–16, soft hits for the words.

Micro-flashes of holographic fragments: signal bars, a glass orb node, `when enjoyment ≥ 1.7`, the decision pill "Happy start matched", a Clerk ✓, the avatar wink. Each uses an extreme angle with streaks, accelerating; warp speed lines grow.
- **30–32:** Silence. Everything collapses to a single glint at centre; a tiny tracked-out "INTRODUCING" label; a flare slowly charges. A `suck_in` ends exactly at 860.
